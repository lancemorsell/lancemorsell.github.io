Lance Morsell, AICP, is an urban planner, economic development professional, and facilitator with experience helping communities navigate growth, redevelopment, and organizational challenges.

Prior to launching his consulting practice, Lance spent six years working in economic development and planning roles in Metro Atlanta, including positions with the City of Doraville and the City of Alpharetta. His work focused on redevelopment initiatives, economic development strategy, land use planning, development review, and stakeholder engagement within some of the Southeast's most dynamic and rapidly growing communities.

Lance later joined the Urban Land Institute, where he served as Director of the ULI Atlanta Technical Assistance Program. In this role, he led the delivery of Technical Assistance Panels and strategic advisory initiatives addressing topics including downtown revitalization, housing, economic development, redevelopment, organizational effectiveness, and community planning. He managed multidisciplinary teams of industry experts while overseeing project management, stakeholder engagement, facilitation, and report development.

Today, Lance operates an independent planning and strategy consulting practice serving clients throughout the United States. While he continues to support Urban Land Institute District Councils on Technical Assistance Panels and advisory projects, his work extends beyond the ULI network to include local governments, economic development organizations, downtown development authorities, universities, nonprofit organizations, and other mission-driven institutions.

Based in State College, Pennsylvania, Lance regularly works on projects throughout the Washington, D.C. metropolitan area, the Mid-Atlantic region, and communities across the country. His approach combines the perspectives of a planner, economic developer, facilitator, and strategic advisor to help communities create practical solutions and implement lasting change.
